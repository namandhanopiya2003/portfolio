Speech to Text Conversion

A speech-to-text system that transcribes audio from a microphone or file using Wav2Vec2 and Whisper. It enables real-time transcription for demos, voice-based AI apps, and quick prototyping of voice-controlled systems.

Challenges Faced

Managing background noise and unclear speech was difficult. I tested various models and audio formats to improve accuracy. Ensuring smooth, real-time transcription with minimal delay required multiple adjustments to balance speed and performance.

What I Learned

I learned how transformer-based speech recognition works, how to fine-tune models for different accents or topics, and how to add voice input to Python applications using pre-trained NLP models.
