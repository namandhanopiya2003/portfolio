TouchlessDocs

A touchless PDF viewer designed for field and lab use. It supports gesture control, voice commands, and real-time display, making it easy and hygienic to view documents without physical contact in sensitive environments.

Challenges Faced

Building a stable gesture recognition system in changing lighting conditions was challenging. Improving speech accuracy in noisy areas and ensuring smooth hands-free control took several testing rounds and a mix of rule-based and ML methods.

What I Learned

I improved my skills in real-time computer vision, system integration, and asynchronous processing. I also learned how to balance speed, accuracy, and user experience while building Python systems for low-resource or hardware-constrained environments.
