TyreMonitor

A smart Android application built to assess and predict tyre health for heavy-duty vehicles using on-device machine learning. It reads structured tyre data, processes it through an optimized model, and presents clear health indicators to support safer operations and proactive maintenance decisions.

Challenges Faced

Integrating machine learning models into an Android environment while maintaining real-time responsiveness was challenging, especially when working with structured JSON data and ensuring predictions were accurate on-device. Optimizing the TFLite model for performance and handling diverse tyre data patterns from multiple heavy-duty vehicles required repeated preprocessing, testing, and model refinement.

What I Learned

I gained hands-on experience deploying ML models to mobile platforms and learned how to preprocess industrial sensor data for robust predictions. I also learned best practices for combining Python-based training pipelines with Kotlin-based Android apps, enabling seamless on-device inference while maintaining user-friendly visualization and actionable tyre health insights.
