Blockchain Powered Secure Payment Simulation

A blockchain-based secure payment simulation designed to demonstrate safe digital transactions using smart contracts, encryption, and threat detection. It combines automated logging, attack simulation, and AI-driven monitoring to show how modern systems can ensure integrity, transparency, and resilience against emerging risks.

Challenges Faced

Coordinating blockchain interactions with real-time encryption and threat analysis was challenging, especially when simulating attacks without interrupting the transaction workflow. Maintaining consistent model confidence while handling irregular patterns and large log files required multiple iterations of tuning, profiling, and refining the data pipeline.

What I Learned

I improved my understanding of integrating blockchain logic with Python systems, combining smart contracts, encryption, and ML-driven threat detection in one workflow. I also learned how to balance security, transparency, and performance while designing a simulation that visualizes behavior under stress and adapts through dynamic scan predictions.
